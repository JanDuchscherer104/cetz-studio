# Evidence and geometry

Classify construction and evidential role separately. Construction may be
schematic, measured, simulated or reconstructed. Role may be a conceptual
contract, hypothesis, qualitative evidence or analyzed result. A measured scene
can illustrate a proposed method without establishing that it works.

For each important visual relationship, identify its source and encoding.
Architecture arrows need actual interfaces or an explicit proposal; names alone
do not establish execution order, tensor shapes or information availability.
A display subset is not the population used for a metric. Invalid samples stay
outside scalar utility color scales and retain an explicit invalid category.

## Spatial inputs

Record frame/basis, handedness, units, projection, camera/up convention,
visibility/clipping rules, input identity and deterministic sampling/decimation
where applicable. Distinguish display-camera edits from measured acquisition
poses. Never replace calibration with a generic icon when claiming measured
geometry. A simplified display mesh is distinct from metric geometry.

A 2D drag does not specify a unique 3D displacement. Use declared display
controls or an explicit axis/plane constraint rather than inventing an inverse.
When occlusion hides a relationship, pair the oblique view with a suitable
orthographic or complete-domain view. Keep comparisons registered in projection,
viewport, fitting/crop and scales; matching azimuth alone is insufficient.

For directional data, identify whether points represent camera positions,
optical axes or ray/surface hits. A direction sphere discards translation.
Show an equal-area complete-domain map when directional density/coverage is the
quantity. Do not invent a smooth field, harmonic lobe or interpolation for a
stored set of discrete directions. State representation invariances and losses.

## Reproducible artifacts

Keep generation source/command, stable input IDs or checksums, dependency
versions, camera/projection/viewport, scales/palette, sampling seed, output mode
and overlay paths with the actual figure/evidence owner. A nominal SVG may
contain a raster 3D base; describe the composition accurately. Keep source text,
mathematics and semantic overlays editable where practical.

Use perceptually ordered scales for ordered quantities; reinforce important
categories with stroke, shape or labels. Compare grayscale and final-size
legibility. Obtain independent review for scientific changes through the
consumer's review owner; passing compilation cannot release a claim.
